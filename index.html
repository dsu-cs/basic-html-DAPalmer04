Sanrio

Sanrio is a Japanese company that designs and produces adorable characters. Most notable being Hello Kitty!
Some Fun Facts:

  • Sanrio's first character was Coro-Chan, a brown and yellow bear. He debuted in 1973, two years before Hello Kitty.
  • Before releasing their own characters, the company was initally a silk company, that later switched over to making sandels, and partnered with the Peanuts to use Snoopy in their products.
  • Sanrio is derived from the Spanish words "San" and "Rio" to have the meaning of "Scared River", a very peaceful name.
